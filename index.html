Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes, for personal use. Run a local model through Ollama at no cost, or bring your own API keys (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, OpenRouter) and pay that provider. Hosted plans start at $8 a month if you want one setup instead. See the free AI interview assistant page for exactly what you get free here.

Does it work with Zoom, Teams, and Google Meet?

Yes. It captures your computer's audio, so it works with Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Slack, Discord, and any other desktop app that plays the call's sound.

Is my data safe?

It is built privacy-first: transcripts, embeddings, and keys stay on your machine. Only product-usage events (which features were used) are reported — never what was said or shown.

Is it truly undetectable?

Yes. It runs entirely on your machine and captures system audio directly. It does not join calls as a bot, extension, or browser plugin, so it is invisible to other participants.

Can I use it for technical interviews?

Yes — coding, system design, and behavioral rounds are first-class modes. Users are responsible for complying with their company policies and interview guidelines.

How is this different from Otter or Fireflies?

Otter and Fireflies join a call with a bot by default. This copilot never does: it listens on your own machine, so no recorder appears in the participant list, and it suggests answers while the call is running — not only notes afterwards.
