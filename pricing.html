What Pro includes.

Modes Manager

Nine built-in modes — Technical Interview, Sales, Recruiting, Lecture and more — or one you make yourself, with its own files to answer from.

Profile Intelligence

Reads your resume and the job description, so every answer draws on what you have done and what the role asks for. It researches the company for you too.

Diagrams

Any diagram, drawn inside the app. Flowcharts, sequences, architectures, timelines, and charts appear inside the answer itself.

Negotiation Assistance

Live salary coaching with real-time counters and anchor strategies based on current market bands.

Is it really free?

Yes. Connect your own API keys, or run a local model through Ollama, and pay nothing. You only pay if you want hosted AI, transcription and search, or the Pro app.

Can I cancel?

Yes — monthly plans cancel anytime. The yearly licence renews yearly; the lifetime licence never renews.

Does the lifetime licence include updates?

Yes — all Pro features and all future updates, for one payment of $79.
