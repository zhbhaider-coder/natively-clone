Natively vs Final Round AI, Cluely, and Parakeet

Four tools do roughly the same job — listen to a live interview and suggest answers in real time — but they charge very different amounts for it, and their free tiers are not the same thing. This page compares them honestly: where a price or feature comes from each company's own pages (October 2026), it says so; where we could not verify a claim, it is marked unverified.

Prices change. Check the vendor's own pricing page before you buy. This is an unofficial rebuild's comparison, not affiliated with Natively or any competitor.

The short version

  • Cheapest way in: Natively — free forever with your own API keys or a local model, or $8/month hosted. Final Round AI starts at $25/month billed yearly ($90 month-to-month); Parakeet AI at $49.99/month billed yearly; Cluely at $149.99/month for Pro with Undetectability.
  • Free live sessions: only Natively's free tier includes them. Final Round AI's free plan has no live sessions; Parakeet AI gives you a first mock interview free; Cluely's free Starter has limited responses.
  • Hidden from screen share: included on every Natively plan; Pro-only at Final Round AI and Cluely; Unlimited-plan only at Parakeet AI.
  • Pay once, own it: Natively offers a $79 lifetime Pro licence. None of the three competitors in this comparison offers a lifetime plan for their copilot (Interview Coder, a related tool, lists a $799 lifetime option).

Natively vs Final Round AI

Final Round AI is the best-known interview copilot brand, and the most expensive way to start: $25 a month billed yearly, or $90 if you pay month to month. Its free plan exists but switches live sessions off — the feature you are buying the tool for. Natively's free tier keeps live sessions on, because it costs Natively nothing when you bring your own key or run a local model.

Where Final Round AI earns its price is polish and interview-specific structure: it is built around interview preparation end to end, including mock interviews and resume tools. Unverified: we have not independently tested its answer speed or accuracy against Natively's, so we make no claim there. If you want the cheapest live copilot, Natively wins on price; if you want a guided interview-prep suite and are happy to pay $25+ a month for it, Final Round AI is the established choice. See Natively pricing for the full tier list.

Natively vs Cluely

Cluely is the tool this whole category is named after — the "invisible AI" that works across apps, not just interviews. Its pitch is undetectability everywhere, and it charges for it: $149.99 a month for Pro with Undetectability, the tier that hides the overlay from screen shares. Its free Starter tier has limited responses.

Natively hides its overlay from screen shares on every plan, including free, and its most expensive managed tier is $35 a month — less than a quarter of Cluely's Pro price. What Cluely offers that Natively does not is breadth: it is a general invisible assistant for any app, not a meeting and interview specialist. Unverified: we have not tested Cluely's detection claims or its answer quality head to head. If your use is interviews and meetings, Natively covers it for far less; if you want an invisible assistant across everything you do, Cluely is the broader tool — at the broader price.

Natively vs Parakeet AI

Parakeet AI sits between the two on price at $49.99 a month billed yearly, and its free tier is a single first mock interview — a taste, not a working tool. Stealth (hidden from screen share) is reserved for its Unlimited plan. It runs on macOS, Windows, and Chrome on Linux, the widest platform spread of the four.

Natively undercuts it sixfold at the entry tier ($8 a month hosted, $0 with your own keys) and includes stealth everywhere. Unverified: Parakeet AI's answer speed, transcription quality, and interview-mode depth were not independently tested for this comparison. If you interview on Linux via Chrome, Parakeet AI is the only one of the four with a stated Linux option; otherwise the price gap is the story.

Which one should you pick?

  • On a budget, or just trying the idea: Natively — the free tier is a real, working copilot, not a demo. Try the live demo or read the free AI interview assistant page.
  • You want a guided prep suite and will pay for it: Final Round AI.
  • You want an invisible assistant for everything, not just interviews: Cluely.
  • You interview on Linux via Chrome: Parakeet AI is the stated option.

Whatever you pick, read what an AI interview copilot is first — especially the ethics note: many employers treat undisclosed live help as cheating, so check the interviewer's policy before using any of these tools in a live interview.
